Pour Company Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Pour Company App is a social app for checking in drinks with photos and sharing them with friends. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. The app is operated by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME]. Contact: [YOUR EMAIL].

Who the app is for

Pour Company contains references to alcohol and is intended only for people who are of legal drinking age in their country. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18, and if we learn we have, we will delete it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, a display name, and a password (stored securely by our authentication provider; we never see it in plain text). We also generate an invite code for your profile.
  • Photos: pictures you take in the app with your camera for check-ins. The app does not read your photo library.
  • Check-in content: the drink you choose, captions, comments, reactions ("Cheers"), and any song you attach to a check-in.
  • Location: if you allow it, the location of a check-in (coordinates and a place label), used to show your check-ins on a map and count cities visited. You can deny location access and still use the app.
  • Dive bar map content: bars you add and ratings or comments you write about them.
  • Friends: friend requests, accepted friendships, and blocks.
  • Reports: posts or comments you report, so we can review them.

How we use information

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your feed and profile, post check-ins, and power leaderboards and maps.
  • To show your check-ins, profile stats, ratings, and display name to other users (see "What others can see").
  • To keep the community safe by reviewing reports and enforcing blocks.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

What others can see

Check-ins (photo, drink, caption, and location label), your display name, your leaderboard stats, and dive bar ratings are visible to other signed-in users of the app. Photo links are public to anyone who has the link. Do not post anything you want to keep private.

Services we use

  • Supabase hosts our database, authentication, and photo storage.
  • Spotify and Apple (iTunes Search) are used to search for songs to attach to a check-in. Only your search terms are sent.
  • Expo and Apple provide app delivery and distribution (TestFlight and the App Store).

These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

Blocking and reporting

You can report posts or comments and block other users from inside the app. Reports are reviewed, and content that violates our rules may be removed and accounts may be suspended.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account, profile, check-ins, photos, and friendships. If you cannot access the app, email pourcompanyapp@icloud.com and we will delete your account.

Data retention and security

We keep your information while your account is active. We use reasonable measures to protect it, including encrypted connections and access rules on our database, but no system is perfectly secure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
